### FAQ 1: Who Can Use Aasirbad?

**Q:** Who can use Aasirbad?

**A:** Anyone who wants to preserve a loved one's voice. Whether you're 
a child recording a parent's blessing, a parent preserving a grandparent's 
stories, or a grandchild wanting to hear their grandfather's voice—Aasirbad

You need just a smartphone, tablet, or computer and an internet connection

**Keywords:** Easy to use, accessible, family app

---

### FAQ 2: Is It Really Free?

**Q:** Is Aasirbad free? What's the catch?

**A:** Yes, Aasirbad is genuinely free to start. You can record, store, 
and share up to [X hours] of voice completely free.

If you want more storage or advanced features (like AI-enhanced transcripts, 
professional editing), we offer a paid plan. But the core—preserving your 
loved one's voice—stays free.

No ads. No upselling. No tracking your data.

**Keywords:** Freemium, affordable, transparent pricing

---

### FAQ 3: How Long Are Recordings Kept?

**Q:** How long will my recordings be stored? Forever?

**A:** Yes. As long as your account is active, your recordings are kept 
safely. We commit to preserving them indefinitely.

We use automatic backups and redundancy to ensure your voice never gets lost, 
even if our company changes.

**Keywords:** Permanent storage, legacy preservation, long-term commitment

---

### FAQ 4: What if Someone Misuses My Recording?

**Q:** What if someone shares my recording without my permission?

**A:** Your recordings are private by default. Only people you explicitly 
share with can access them. You control every single share link.

If you suspect misuse, you can revoke access immediately. If someone uses 
your voice without consent for commercial purposes, we have legal protections 
in place to help you.

**Keywords:** Privacy control, consent, legal protection

---

### FAQ 5: Can I Edit or Delete Recordings?

**Q:** Can I edit my recording after uploading? Can I delete it?

**A:** Yes to both.

You can trim recordings, add notes, or adjust privacy settings anytime. 
If you want to delete a recording, it's gone immediately from our servers 
(and all backups within 30 days).

No questions asked. No judgment. Your choice, always.

**Keywords:** User control, flexibility, ownership

### FAQ 6: What About Transcripts & Translation?

**Q:** Can Aasirbad transcribe or translate my voice?

**A:** Yes. We offer AI-powered transcripts in the original language 
(Nepali, Hindi, English, etc.) and can translate to other languages.

This is optional. Transcripts are also private and only accessible to 
people who have access to the recording.

**Keywords:** Multilingual support, accessibility, transcription

**Q:** What devices work with Aasirbad?

Record on your smartphone (iOS/Android), tablet, laptop, or desktop.

## 🎨 UI/UX RECOMMENDATIONS

### Homepage Structure (Recommended Flow)

```
1. HERO (Emotional hook + immediate CTA)
   ↓
2. TRUST BADGES (Micro-copy: "Free to start | Your data | Secure")
   ↓
3. WHAT IS AASIRBAD? (3-column benefits)
   ↓
4. HOW IT WORKS (3 steps, visual diagram)
   ↓
5. USE CASES (4 real stories, carousel or full-width)
   ↓
6. TRUST & PRIVACY (4 pillars with icons)
   ↓
7. FAQ (Accordion, searchable)
   ↓
8. FINAL CTA + Social Proof
   ↓
9. FOOTER
```

### Design Principles

#### Color Palette (Warm, Trustworthy)
- **Primary:** Warm Gold (#D4A574) or Warm Brown (#8B6F47)
- **Secondary:** Soft Teal/Blue (#6B9BD1) - Trust color
- **Accent:** Rose/Mauve (#C9989A) - Emotion, family
- **Neutral:** Warm White (#F8F6F1), Deep Charcoal (#2C2C2C)

#### Typography
- **Headlines:** Serif font (Georgia, Crimson, Playfair) - Timeless, classic
- **Body:** Clean sans-serif (Inter, Poppins, Manrope) - Modern, readable
- **Emotional copy:** 16-18px, generous line-height (1.6-1.8)

#### Visual Elements
- Authentic family photography (multigenerational, diverse)
- Hand-drawn icons (not corporate stock icons)
- Waveform visuals (represent voice/audio)
- Warm gradients and overlays
- Lots of whitespace (breathing room)

#### Microinteractions
- Hover states on buttons (color shift, subtle animation)
- FAQ accordion opens smoothly
- CTA buttons have clear focus states (accessibility)
- Scroll-triggered animations (subtle fade-in for sections)

- Not about "AI voice cloning" (feels eerie)
- It's about "preserving real memories in real voices"
- Not about "technology," it's about "family and legacy"
- Tone: Warm and human, not corporate

### Cultural Sensitivity Checklist
- ✓ Include multigenerational imagery
- ✓ Honor traditional values (blessings, wisdom, family hierarchy)
- ✓ Use culturally relevant examples (Partition stories, diaspora families)
- ✓ Translate fully, don't just copy-paste English
- ✓ Include Nepali, Hindi, regional languages naturally
- ✓ Acknowledge the spiritual/emotional significance of voice

- Test hero headlines: Emotional vs. Functional
- Test CTA text: "Start Free" vs. "Record Now" vs. "Preserve Forever"
- Test use case imagery: Professional photos vs. authentic family photos
- Test FAQ placement: Hero-level vs. Bottom of page
